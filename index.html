Welcome to my homepage! I received my B.S. in Electronic Engineering (2024) and M.S. in Semiconductor Engineering (2026) from Kyung Hee University, advised by Prof. Seungkyu Choi in the ACA Lab (now Yonsei).

I am interested in co-optimizing hardware and algorithms to build low-latency, energy-efficient computing systems for AI and data-intensive workloads. My research focuses on memory-centric architectures and dedicated hardware systems specialized for vector similarity search, with an emphasis on minimizing data movement.
